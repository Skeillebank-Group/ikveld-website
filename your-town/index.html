What happens in a new town

The first plan there takes a little longer. Every plan after that is quick, for everyone.

It looks the town up

The first time someone plans somewhere new, iKveld looks up the restaurants, bars and things to do nearby. That's the slow part.

It keeps what it found

The town is added for good, so the next person who plans there gets their plans straight away.

It says so

If iKveld found only a few places near you, the plan says so, and offers the nearest town with more to choose from. No pretending.

Somebody has to be first in your town. It may as well be you.

Your town doesn't need a waiting list. It needs one person to plan a night out there.

Getting iKveld in your town

  • Anywhere in the UK, there's nothing to fill in. Your town is added the first time someone plans a night out there.
  • Going further? A weekend away works across Europe and further afield: Berlin, Barcelona, Prague. Outside the UK and Ireland, a city isn't added for good, so each plan there looks it up afresh and takes a little longer.
  • Before the app opens in December, email us your town with the button above. We'll email you when the app opens, so you can plan the first night out there.

You know the good places in your town. iKveld is still learning them.
