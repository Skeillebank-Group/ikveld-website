A whole evening, not just a restaurant

Most of the work of a night out is the planning. iKveld does it, and shows its reasons.

1 · Tell it

Where, when, what you like

Your starting point, the date, a budget per person and how far you'll go. Add what you like (Italian, comedy, a walk, a film), or nothing at all.

2 · Get two or three plans

Each one complete

Somewhere to eat and something to do after, with the cost, the weather, a backup if it rains, and when to leave. Every place and event is real, with a link to its source.

3 · Agree and go

Send it, pick one, book it

Send the plans to your partner or your group. Pick together, add it to your calendar, and book with the restaurant.

For the two of you, and for nights out with friends

Couples

Link up with your partner and plan for both of you.

  • Plans use what you both like, and skip places either of you didn't.
  • Take turns to pick; say why when you'd rather go somewhere else.
  • Mark the evenings you're free and see when you both are.

Friends

Start a group, share the link, and let everyone vote.

  • Plans for the whole group, with the cost for everyone.
  • Dinner, drinks, or a pub crawl: three or four bars a short walk apart, with a time for each stop.
  • Most of you saying yes makes it on.
  • Everyone sees how many are free and how many voted, never who.

Going away?

Plan a weekend: the hotel, then dinner, drinks or a pub crawl for each night, never the same place twice. In Belfast, Dublin, Galway or further afield. Send it to your partner or the group to agree.

Made to be trusted

  • Nothing invented. Every restaurant and event comes from a real source, with a link, and every plan shows why it was picked.
  • No ads, no tracking. Paid placements, if there ever are any, are always labelled.
  • Little kept, for a short time. Your starting point is never stored, and what you share with a partner or a group goes once the evening has passed.
  • Free to start. Your first plan needs no account, and a free account covers your date nights each month.

For restaurants, venues and partners

Every iKveld plan sends people to a specific place on a specific evening. We'd like that to end in a booking: a table, tickets for after, or somewhere to stay on a weekend away. Booking and partner links are always labelled.

If you run a restaurant, a venue or a booking service in the UK or Ireland and would like to work with us, write to hello@ikveld.com.
