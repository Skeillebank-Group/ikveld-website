A whole evening, not just a restaurant

Most of the work of a date night is the planning. iKveld does it, and shows its reasons.

1. Tell it

Where, when, what you like

Your starting point, the date, a budget per person and how far you'll travel. Add what you like, or nothing at all.

2. Get two or three plans

Each one complete

Somewhere to eat and something to do before or after, with the cost, the weather, a backup if it rains, and when to set off. Each place links to its own website or map.

3. Agree and go

Send it, pick one, book it

Send the plans to your partner. Pick one together, add it to your calendar, and book with the restaurant.

The hard part of a date night was never the dinner. It was deciding where.

For the two of you

Couples

Link up with your partner and plan for both of you.

  • Plans use what you both like, and skip places either of you didn't.
  • Take turns to pick; say why when you'd rather go somewhere else.
  • Mark the evenings you're free and see when you both are.
  • Keep it a surprise: your partner sees only the kind of food and the price until the day.
  • Set a regular date night, every week or once a month, and iKveld plans each one (iKveld Plus).

You bring the company. iKveld makes the plan.

And for nights out with friends, or a weekend away

Friends

Start a group, share the link, and let everyone vote.

  • Plans for the whole group, with the cost for everyone.
  • Dinner, drinks, or a pub crawl: three or four bars a short walk apart, with a time for each stop.
  • If most of you say yes, the night's on.
  • Everyone sees how many are free and how many voted, never who.

What iKveld leaves out

  • Made-up places

    Every restaurant, bar and event comes from a real source, with a link, and every plan shows why it was picked.

  • Ads and tracking

    No adverts and no tracking. If a place ever pays to be shown, it's labelled, and paying never moves it up.

  • A record of your nights out

    Your starting point is never stored, and what you share with a partner or a group is deleted once the evening has passed.

For restaurants, venues and partners

Every iKveld plan sends people to a specific place on a specific evening. We'd like that to end in a booking: a table, tickets for after, or somewhere to stay on a weekend away. Booking and partner links are always labelled.

If you run a restaurant, a venue or a booking service in the UK or Ireland and would like to work with us, write to hello@ikveld.com.
