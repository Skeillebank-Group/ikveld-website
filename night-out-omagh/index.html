What a night out in Omagh looks like in iKveld

Start a group, share the link, and iKveld does the rest.

Dinner for the group

Somewhere that suits everyone, with the cost for each person shown before anyone says yes.

Or a pub crawl

Three or four bars a short walk apart, with a time for each stop.

Everyone votes

Share the link and let the group vote. Most of you saying yes makes it on. Everyone sees how many voted, never who.

All you have to do is turn up.

Six people, one group chat, forty messages. Or one link and a vote.

Good to know in Omagh

  • Omagh's town centre is compact, so dinner and a few drinks after are never far apart.
  • Everyone sees the cost per person before they say yes, so nobody gets a surprise at the bar.
