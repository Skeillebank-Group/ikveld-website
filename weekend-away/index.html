A weekend in Berlin? iKveld plans that too.

iKveld starts with date nights in Omagh, Enniskillen, Derry and Strabane. A weekend away can be somewhere else entirely: Belfast, Dublin, Berlin, Barcelona, Prague, or further afield. Say where and when, and iKveld plans the whole weekend, days and nights.

What a weekend away looks like in iKveld

One plan for the whole weekend, up to 4 nights, not a pile of browser tabs. Send it to your partner or the group to agree.

Somewhere to stay

A hotel near the centre: simple, mid-range or a treat, your call. Spa hotels come first if you want one. Bringing the campervan? It finds a campsite, and keeps the meals on foot or a taxi away so the van stays put. Already booked or staying with friends? It plans around that.

Every meal and every night

Brunch, lunch, dinner, drinks or a pub crawl, on the days you choose, planned from where you're staying. Never the same place twice.

Afternoons out

A museum, a castle, a distillery, a spa, bowling, an escape room or a walk to a waterfall. If the afternoon looks wet, indoor ideas come first.

All you have to do is pack.

You don't need to know Berlin. You need to know you want to go.

Good to know

  • It works across Europe and further afield. Outside the UK and Ireland, iKveld looks the city up from the map each time, so the plan takes a little longer. You can close the app; it tells you when your weekend is ready.
  • Going somewhere often? Tap "I want iKveld here" on the plan. The cities people ask for most are the ones we add next.
  • Abroad, prices are in the local currency, and the plan shows your budget converted at the European Central Bank's rate. Your £40 each becomes about €47, not a guess.

The planning is the chore. The weekend isn't.

Closer to home

iKveld also plans date nights and nights out with friends, starting in Omagh, Enniskillen, Derry and Strabane. Somewhere else? It still plans.
