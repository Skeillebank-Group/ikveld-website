Date night in Derry/Londonderry, planned.

You both pick. iKveld plans the whole night. Derry/Londonderry packs the walls, the river and a lot of good food into a small space. That makes for a great date night, once somebody decides where. iKveld decides, and shows you why.

What a date night in Derry/Londonderry looks like in iKveld

Two or three complete plans, each with its reasons. Send them to your partner and pick one together.

Somewhere to eat

A table that suits you both, within your budget. Places either of you didn't like are left out.

Something before or after

A film, a show, a walk or a drink nearby, so the evening is more than the meal.

The practical bits

What it'll cost for two, the weather, a backup if it rains, and when to set off.

All you have to do is turn up.

Keep it a surprise, or keep it regular

Keep it a surprise

Your partner sees only the kind of food and the price, or just the time, until the day. They can always tap "Show me where".

A regular date night

Every week, every other week, or once a month. iKveld plans each one a few days before, and you take turns to pick. With iKveld Plus.

Good to know in Derry/Londonderry

  • The Millennium Forum and the Guildhall host shows and concerts, a few minutes' walk from the city centre's restaurants.
  • A walk over the Peace Bridge is a good way to end the evening, and it costs nothing.
