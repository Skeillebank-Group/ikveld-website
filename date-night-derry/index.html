Date night in Derry, planned.

Derry packs the walls, the river and a lot of good food into a small space. That makes for a great date night, once somebody decides where. iKveld decides, and shows you why.

What a date night in Derry looks like in iKveld

Two or three complete plans, each with its reasons. Send them to your partner and pick one together.

Somewhere to eat

A table that suits you both, within your budget and how far you're happy to travel. Places either of you didn't like are left out.

Something after

A film, a show, a walk or a drink nearby, so the evening doesn't end at the bill.

The practical bits

What it'll cost for two, the weather, a backup if it rains, and when to leave.

Every place is real, with a link to where it came from.

“Where do you fancy?” “I don't mind, you pick.” iKveld picks, and tells you why.

Good to know in Derry

  • The Millennium Forum and the Guildhall host shows and concerts, a few minutes' walk from the city centre's restaurants.
  • A walk over the Peace Bridge is a good way to end the evening, and it costs nothing.
